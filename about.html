About the dictionary

A free, lightweight multilingual dictionary for linguists and polyglots who want definitions, forms, etymology and cross-language connections without an account. Search in Russian, Mandarin Chinese, French, Catalan, Spanish, Portuguese or English.

Anki, optional

Use Save cards to keep notes in this browser. Turn on Auto-add to save each lookup and its available partners. Neither requires an Anki connection.

For direct transfer, install Anki Desktop and AnkiConnect, restart Anki, then choose Connect Anki here. Approve this specific website in Anki and any local-network browser prompt. Keep AnkiConnect on 127.0.0.1; never expose it to the internet or allow every website.

If that connection is blocked, use Export saved cards and import the TSV into Anki with HTML enabled. The nine columns are Word, Language, Meaning, Definition, Pronunciation, Forms, Example, Notes and Source. A custom note type keeps all nine; Basic can import Word as Front and Meaning as Back. Mobile browsers cannot reach Anki on another computer. Export does not erase saved notes.

Linguistic tools

Contrast mode lets you pin one sourced entry and compare it with another. Grammar frames are conservative restatements of explicit Wiktionary labels such as transitive, intransitive, countability, reflexive/impersonal/copular use and explicit case government. Collocations appear only when Wiktionary has an explicit collocations section. Across your languages shows terms from explicit translation sections and a separately labeled Shared history group from etymology links. These features use no AI service, account or API key, and missing evidence is omitted rather than generated.

Pronunciation audio

Choose Listen beside the pronunciation. More recordings and credits are in the adjacent disclosure. Playback is for the displayed headword, not necessarily the inflected form you searched. Regional labels appear only when stated by the source. Unverified attribution, ambiguous homographs and unsupported language-variety associations are omitted; request failures can be retried. Chinese variety-specific audio is not yet mapped safely. No audio files are downloaded until you choose to play.

Know the limits

Language detection is a suggestion, not certainty. Use the alternate-language buttons when a word is ambiguous. Inflection and accent recovery depend on the source. Pronunciation, target-language definitions and related-word notes may be missing or imperfect. Frequency bands reflect subtitle word forms, not language proficiency. Consult source entries before relying on a grammatical claim.

Sources & licenses

Entries link to Wiktionary and its target-language editions. Their contributors’ text is extracted and reformatted under the source’s applicable ShareAlike terms (normally CC BY-SA 4.0; see each page’s footer and history). Optional Wikipedia summaries link to the source article and retain its terms. Images are not embedded. Optional human pronunciation recordings are linked from the selected Wiktionary pronunciation section and played from Wikimedia Commons only after their individual creator and license metadata are verified. Open Recordings & credits for the exact creator, source file and license of each recording. No autoplay, synthesis or blanket Wikimedia media license is used.

Tatoeba sentences link to the original and translated sentence, with author and reported license. Frequency ranks come from the 2018 FrequencyWords/OpenSubtitles lists. Third-party licenses are not replaced by this project. Full source notes ↗

Privacy

This app adds no analytics or account system. Your browser contacts Wikimedia, Tatoeba and GitHub directly, so those providers receive requests and their usual metadata. GitHub Pages is the host and has its own privacy policy. Search history, cached entries and saved cards stay in this browser’s local storage. There is no cross-device sync; clearing site data removes them. Export cards before clearing data.
